1. Developer & Application Identity

This Privacy Policy outlines how An Intellectual Company ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") protects and respects your privacy when you install and play Arrow Twist (Package Name: com.anintellectualcompany.arrowtwist).

Arrow Twist is a free-to-play puzzle game supported by advertisements. We are committed to transparency and upholding user privacy standards across all global regions including the United States, European Union (GDPR), United Kingdom (UK GDPR), California (CCPA/CPRA), and Brazil (LGPD).

2. Information We Collect

To provide free gameplay and support app development through advertising, Arrow Twist and our third-party advertising partners automatically collect certain technical and device data when you use the app.

A. Information Collected Automatically by Ad Partners:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) or Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).
  • Technical Device Information: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, screen resolution, system language, and network connection type.
  • Location Data: Coarse/approximate location derived from your device's IP address (used to serve geo-relevant ads and ensure ad fraud prevention).
  • Ad Performance Data: Ad views, clicks, impressions, frequency capping metrics, and diagnostic error reports.

B. Information We Do NOT Collect Directly:

  • We do not collect personal identity details such as your real name, email address, phone number, physical address, or contact lists.
  • We do not require user registration or account creation.

5. Local Data Storage & Preferences

To preserve your puzzle progress across gameplay sessions, Arrow Twist uses Android's local preferences storage (Jetpack DataStore Preferences).

Data stored locally includes:

  • Unlocked level index and highest completed campaign stage.
  • In-game audio, music, and vibration preference toggles.
  • Best move count or timer high scores for solved levels.
  • Consent choice preferences (e.g. GDPR consent selection).

This data resides exclusively on your physical mobile device within the application's isolated sandbox storage.

6. Children's Privacy Compliance (COPPA & Google Play Families)

Arrow Twist is designed for general audiences. We comply fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), EU GDPR Article 8, and Google Play Families Policy.

  • If a user is determined to be under 13 (or under 16 in the EU), our ad networks serve non-personalized, contextual ads only.
  • No behavioral profiling or interest-based tracking is performed on child users.
  • Parents or guardians with questions regarding child privacy may contact us at privacy@anintellectualcompany.com.

7. Your Privacy Rights & Opt-Out Controls

Under privacy regulations such as GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and CalOPPA, you have full control over your advertising data preferences:

A. Opting Out of Interest-Based Ads on Android:

  • Open Android Device Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads.
  • Tap Delete Advertising ID or toggle Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • When opted out, ad networks will continue to show ads, but they will be non-personalized contextual ads.

B. GDPR / CCPA Consent Management:

European Union and California residents are presented with a consent choice dialog upon first launch to accept or customize ad personalization options.

C. Erasing Local Game Data:

You can clear all saved game progress via Android Settings > Apps > Arrow Twist > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
